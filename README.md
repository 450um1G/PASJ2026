# BISTRO 450/850 µm polarimetry series: manuscripts submitted to PASJ

This repository holds the three papers of the BISTRO 450/850 µm polarimetry series as submitted to *Publications of the Astronomical Society of Japan* (PASJ).
The papers were submitted on 2026 October 21, returned by the editorial office before review, and resubmitted with revisions on 2026 October 23. The PDFs here are the resubmitted versions.
They are the Author's Original Versions, that is, the manuscripts before peer review.
The PDFs of 2026 October 21 (GitHub version 1) remain in the history of this repository. On arXiv, Papers T and S1 are the versions of 2026 October 21, and Paper S2 is the version of 2026 October 23.

Authors: Ray S. Furuya et al. and the BISTRO Collaboration. The three papers have different author lists; see the first page of each PDF.

The PDFs carry line and page numbers, as required for review. In the PDF of Paper S2, the images were resampled to 300 ppi to keep the file within the size limit of the PASJ submission system; the text is the same as in the build at full resolution.

Each page carries one line in its bottom margin that names this version: "Resubmitted to PASJ on 2026 October 23 (Author's Original Version, not peer reviewed). GitHub version 2, 2026 November 7: https://github.com/450um1G/PASJ2026". Apart from this line, the PDFs are those uploaded to the PASJ submission system.

When the papers are accepted and published, links to the published versions will be added here.
