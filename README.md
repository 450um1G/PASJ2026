# BISTRO 450/850 µm polarimetry series: manuscripts submitted to PASJ

This repository holds the three papers of the BISTRO 450/850 µm polarimetry series as submitted to *Publications of the Astronomical Society of Japan* (PASJ) on 21 October 2026.
The PDFs are the Author's Original Versions, that is, the manuscripts before peer review.

| Paper | Title | PDF | arXiv |
|---|---|---|---|
| T | B-fields In STar-forming Region Observations (BISTRO): Technical Foundations for Quantitative 450/850 μm JCMT SCUBA-2/POL-2 Polarimetry | [RSFuruya_PaperT_2026Oct21submitted.pdf](./RSFuruya_PaperT_2026Oct21submitted.pdf) (75 pages, 7.7 MB) | to be added |
| S1 | B-fields In STar-forming Region Observations (BISTRO): Dual-Wavelength Constraints on Size-Dependent Grain Alignment in Star-Forming Regions | [RSFuruya_PaperS1_2026Oct21submitted.pdf](./RSFuruya_PaperS1_2026Oct21submitted.pdf) (45 pages, 3.1 MB) | to be added |
| S2 | B-fields In STar-forming Region Observations (BISTRO): Population-Level Universality and Environmental Structuring of 450/850 μm Polarization Signatures | [RSFuruya_PaperS2_2026Oct21submitted.pdf](./RSFuruya_PaperS2_2026Oct21submitted.pdf) (160 pages, 17.5 MB) | to be posted in November 2026 |

Authors: Ray S. Furuya et al. and the BISTRO Collaboration. The three papers have different author lists; see the first page of each PDF.

The PDFs carry line and page numbers, as required for review. In the PDF of Paper S2, the images were resampled to 300 ppi to keep the file within the size limit of the PASJ submission system; the text is the same as in the build at full resolution.

When the papers are accepted and published, links to the published versions will be added here.
